~*~ The Carnivorous Patch ~*~

Most plants are happy with sunshine and water. NOT MINE!!! Mine want MEAT. Well, bugs anyway. Out on my back porch I keep a tub of peat and sand where the hungriest green things on Earth live:
  • Venus Flytraps - the famous ones. Two little jaws with bristly teeth that SNAP when a fly touches the trigger hairs twice.
  • Pitcher Plants - tall slippery tubes. Bugs climb in for the sweet stuff and slide right down into the soup. Yuck and cool at the same time.
  • Sundews - sparkly sticky drops on every leaf, like dew that never dries. A gnat lands, gets stuck, and the leaf slowly curls up around it.
  • Butterworts - flat greasy leaves that work like flypaper. Not fancy, but they get the job done.
Rule #1: Do NOT feed them hamburger! I learned that one the hard way. Rule #2: Only rainwater or distilled water, never tap water. Rule #3: Stop poking the traps to watch them close, Kevin. Every snap costs the plant energy!!



~*~ The Sanctuary Greenhouse ~*~

Lots of these plants are getting rare in the wild because people drain the bogs to build malls and parking lots. :-( So I made my greenhouse into a sanctuary, a safe place where they can grow and make babies.
  • Every plant here was grown from seed or a leaf cutting. NONE were dug up from the wild! Please never, ever do that.
  • My sundews make so many seeds that I trade them by snail mail with other bog growers. Just ask if you want some!
  • In winter the flytraps go to sleep in the cold frame. They are NOT dead, they are just resting, so don't panic like I did my first year.
  • Lost plants are welcome too. If your pitcher plant looks sad and brown, bring it over and it can get better here.
A sanctuary is supposed to be safe for everybody... except the flies. Sorry, flies!
